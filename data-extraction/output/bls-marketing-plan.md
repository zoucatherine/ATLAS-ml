BLS MARKETING PLAN

The following marketing plan outlines the strategies and tactics to recruit prospective and admitted students into the Bachelor of Liberal Studies (BLS) for enrollment starting in Spring 2026. The goal of this marketing plan is twofold: to meet BLS enrollment targets and to enhance the long-term growth and reputation of the College of Liberal Arts & Sciences. By showcasing the unique value of the BLS, we will not only drive enrollment but also contribute to a broader narrative about the value of liberal arts education in today’s job market.

# Program overview

The Bachelor of Liberal Studies is an accessible, flexible, and affordable degree program designed for students who have finished some college coursework but have not yet completed their degree. The program offers a fully online format, making it ideal for students who need the flexibility to balance work, life, and education. It is co-enrolled with current on-campus students, providing learners with the opportunity to engage with a diverse academic community and access resources typically available to on-campus students.

## Program features

Aligns with campus strategic plan to offer transformative learning experiences (“Provide new educational pathways and enhance current programs to increase flexibility and to foster education across disciplines”).

Delivered fully online and mostly asynchronously, offering students the flexibility to learn from anywhere, at any time.

Co-enrolled with on-campus students, fostering a diverse and dynamic learning environment.

Courses are cross-disciplinary with options for students to focus in specialized concentrations:

Global Perspectives

Health & Society

Management Studies

Students have access to robust support services provided by LAS, including admissions assistance, transfer credit evaluations, academic advising, career services, and more.

Because of its low entry point and accessible cost, the BLS  is bridging the equity gap and opening access for marginalized populations.

Maintains the rigor of a University of Illinois degree, ensuring adult learners are earning a quality education that will serve them throughout their lives.

### Entry points

The BLS application is available year-round, with prospects able to submit their application on a monthly basis. With rolling admissions and three start dates each year—Fall, Spring, and Summer—students have the flexibility to begin their studies at a time that best fits their schedule. This approach is especially beneficial for nontraditional learners, who often prefer to pursue higher education outside the traditional academic cycle.

## Needs assessment

The development of the BLS was shaped by key needs identified within the current higher education landscape alongside specific requests from prospective BLS students. Through research and interviews, the BLS team uncovered a need for a flexible, accessible, and valuable program that caters to adult and young adult learners, individuals seeking to complete their degrees, and those aiming to advance their careers.

Flexibility: Many students, particularly working adults and those with family responsibilities, require the flexibility to complete their degree while balancing other life commitments. The BLS is designed to meet this need by offering a fully online, flexible curriculum that can be tailored to fit students’ schedules.

Affordable, high-quality education: As the cost of higher education continues to rise, students are seeking more affordable pathways to degree completion without sacrificing quality. The BLS provides a cost-effective option that enables students to earn their degree at a lower cost than traditional programs.

Career advancement: Individuals are looking for ways to enhance their career mobility and salary potential. The BLS is designed to equip students with skills that directly align with employers’ needs, thus positioning graduates for greater career opportunities.

Transferable skills: Employers are increasingly seeking graduates with skills that go beyond academic knowledge. In response to this demand, the BLS focuses on practical, career-relevant skills with concentrations that are tailored to ensure that students are well-prepared for the modern workforce.

Support: Adult learners often have concerns about returning to school after a break in their education. To address these concerns, the BLS provides comprehensive support services, including personalized advising and direct response from real people.

# Branding

The BLS offers students a unique opportunity to achieve their educational and professional goals through a highly flexible, affordable, and supportive online environment. These attributes should be reflected through visual and written messaging.

## Key messaging themes

Messaging around the BLS should highlight the program’s flexibility while showcasing how it will help with career advancement and personal achievement. It should address the needs of nontraditional learners seeking to further their education without disrupting other commitments.

Career elevation: Career growth and new opportunities come with a completed degree.

Complete your journey, on your terms: You’ll appreciate the flexibility to finish the degree at a reasonable pace but with guardrails to keep you motivated and on track.

Flexible for busy adults: Online, (mostly) asynchronous courses and multiple start dates help fit education into busy lives.

Affordable, achievable: The program is a cost-effective and accessible option.

Supportive environment: Personalized assistance through dedicated advisors and career services ensure success from application to graduation.

Pride in milestones: Personal fulfillment comes with completing an educational journey.

## Key messaging approach

Building on the key messaging themes, we will create collateral that effectively communicates these ideas. A strong focus will be placed on leveraging current students and alumni to share their stories and provide testimonials, helping to authentically highlight the program’s impact. Additionally, we’ll engage corporate partners (see partnerships below) to explain why they value candidates with a BLS degree, further emphasizing the program’s real-world relevance and career benefits.

Showcase return on investment

Improved salary: Data shows that degree holders earn more over their lifetime compared to those without a degree. Messaging should underscore this financial return, showing how the BLS helps students improve their earning potential.

Career mobility: The BLS provides the necessary skills and credentials for career advancement, whether students are looking to transition into a new field or move up within their current organization.

Personal accomplishment: Beyond the professional and financial benefits, the BLS provides students with the opportunity for personal growth.

Highlight market-relevant skills

Communication: The BLS helps students refine their ability to convey ideas clearly, both verbally and in writing, a fundamental skill in nearly every profession.

Leadership & teamwork: Employers look for individuals who can work well with others. The BLS teaches students how to collaborate in diverse team environments.

Critical thinking: The ability to analyze complex situations, make sound decisions, and solve problems is highly valued by employers.

Data analysis: The BLS includes courses that equip students with the knowledge to make informed, data-driven decisions, an essential skill as industries continue to rely on data.

Cultural competency: As workplaces become more global and diverse, understanding different cultures and perspectives is crucial.

Address common nontraditional learner concerns

Lower cost: The BLS offers a lower tuition rate compared to traditional degree programs, allowing students to earn a quality degree without incurring high debt.

Accreditation: The BLS is an accredited program that ensures students will receive a degree that is both high-quality and credible.

Online support services: From personalized advising to career assistance, BLS students can access a robust support system that ensures they’re supported every step of the way.

Flexibility: Online, asynchronous courses and multiple start dates help fit education into busy lives. Students can take just a few courses a semester to keep things manageable.
